I am **Dongyang Chen** (陈东阳), a researcher at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/). My research focuses on **Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)**, with particular interests in **agentic reasoning**, **adaptive tool-use**, and **universal multimodal retrieval**.

I work on training MLLMs to reason over visual evidence in a more reliable and efficient way—rather than relying on static visual encodings or blindly invoking tools. My recent projects include:

- **[V-Retrver](https://github.com/chendy25/V-Retrver)** ([arXiv:2602.06034](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06034)): an evidence-driven agentic reasoning framework for universal multimodal retrieval, where an MLLM selectively acquires visual evidence via external tools during interleaved multimodal reasoning.
- **[AdaTooler-V](https://github.com/chendy25/AdaTooler-V)** ([ACL Findings 2026](https://aclanthology.org/people/dongyang-chen/)): adaptive tool-use for images and videos, encouraging models to invoke vision tools only when they provide genuine improvements.

I am broadly interested in:

- Multimodal / interleaved Chain-of-Thought reasoning
- Reinforcement learning for MLLMs (e.g., GRPO-style optimization with verifiable rewards)
- Agentic systems that combine perception, tool-use, and retrieval
- Building open-source models, datasets, and training pipelines

You can find more of my work on [GitHub](https://github.com/chendy25) and [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/DongyangChen).

Feel free to reach out if you are interested in collaboration or discussion.
